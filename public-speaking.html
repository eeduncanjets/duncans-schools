PRONUNCIATION & ARTICULATION LAB

Train your clarity, one sound at a time

Listen, then repeat

Hear a phrase at a comfortable speed, then repeat it. Browser speech recognition can compare the words it recognises; it cannot reliably grade every accent or phoneme.

Articulation warm-up

Move gently and comfortably. Do not force your jaw or tongue. Repeat each line slowly, then at a natural pace.

  1. Relax your jaw and let your lips move freely.
  2. Say “pa-ba-ma” slowly five times.
  3. Say “ta-da-na” slowly five times.
  4. Read one sentence while finishing each word.
  5. Repeat the sentence with a natural rhythm.
