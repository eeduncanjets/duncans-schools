Learning reference

Open the LC-3 Handbook (PDF)

Lessons and worked examples are organised around the supplied LC-3 Instructions Handbook, including its chapters on architecture, assembly syntax, operate instructions, data movement, control instructions and exercises. Examples are adapted for this browser lab; check the handbook and your instructor's simulator conventions where behaviour differs.

The in-browser corrector is rule-based. It can catch and repair common formatting and label mistakes, but students should still understand and verify every suggested change.
