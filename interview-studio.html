Answer frameworks

STAR

Situation: Set the context. Task: Explain your responsibility. Action: Describe what you did. Result: Share the outcome and what you learned.

Technical explanation

Define the concept, explain how it works, give a practical example, discuss trade-offs and mention how you would test it.

Problem solving

Clarify the requirements, state assumptions, outline an approach, discuss complexity, test edge cases and improve the solution.
