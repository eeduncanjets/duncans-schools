Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, streaks, challenge results, focus sessions, weekly study target, theme choice, playground code, certificate name, Project Lab drafts, Interview Studio practice history, Innovation Academy venture canvas and milestones, and learner XP are saved in your browser's local storage. These new tools do not automatically sync to your account. To remove their locally stored data, clear this site's browser storage in your browser settings.

What we collect

If you subscribe to the newsletter or send a message through the contact form, we receive the details you type (such as your name and email address) through Netlify Forms. We use them only to reply to you or to send the digest you asked for. To be removed, contact us and we will delete your details.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, your email address and your learning progress are stored with our account provider, Supabase, so they can sync across your devices. Passwords are handled by the provider and are never visible to us. You can delete your synced progress on the account page, or ask for full deletion on the contact page.

The study helper

When the AI helper is switched on, the question you type, plus the lesson text and code on your screen, are sent to our server and then to Anthropic to write an answer. Please do not type personal information into the chat. When it is switched off, the built-in helper runs entirely in your browser.

Third-party services

To work, pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr (including the Python and SQL engines). These providers may see your IP address, as with any website. This site is hosted on Netlify.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. If privacy-friendly analytics are switched on in future, this page will say so.

Offline use

The site installs a service worker so lessons can work offline. It stores copies of pages and libraries in your browser cache.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact page.
