REAL-WORLD VALIDATION

Talk to people before you overbuild.

Use this interview guide to test whether the problem is real. Avoid asking leading questions.

Ask about the past

“Tell me about the last time you experienced this problem.”

Understand the impact

“What was the hardest part? How much time, money or effort did it cost?”

Explore workarounds

“What have you already tried? What do you dislike about the current options?”

Look for commitment

“Would you be willing to try an early prototype and give feedback?”

Research ethics: ask permission before recording, collect only information you need, protect personal data, and never present guesses as validated findings.
