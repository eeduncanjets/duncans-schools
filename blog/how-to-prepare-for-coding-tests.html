How to prepare for coding tests and interviews

By Duncan · 18 October 2026 · 4 min read

Coding tests reward practice more than talent. A few weeks of steady, focused work can change your results a lot.

A weekly plan

  • Two short quizzes on the practice tests page. Review every wrong answer.
  • Three small problems from the challenges. Aim to solve them without looking up the answer.
  • One topic revision from the data structures course.

During the test

  1. Read the question twice and restate it in your own words.
  2. Try a tiny example by hand first.
  3. Write a simple solution that works, then improve it.
  4. Test with edge cases: an empty list, one item, negative numbers.
  5. Say your thinking out loud in interviews. Interviewers want to see how you reason.
